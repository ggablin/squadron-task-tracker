The Other 60 Points · Panel-Scored

NJANG IMT 004 · 30 pts
four sections, 7.5 pts max each — completed by your supervisor
Graded on proficiency levels (Developing → Outstanding) against your rank, AFSC, and assigned duties.
Blank or vague sections score 0. Section 2 is awards that are not federal decorations; Section 3 is your responsibilities and leadership experience — additional duties count.
The supervisor endorsement is the “why promote” — and the place for anything not already in the package.
Enlisted Performance Brief · the remaining points
the panel reads it directly — writing quality carries real points
A three-member panel scores this portion from your finalized documents. Work the EPB and IMT 004 with your supervisor before the package goes in — drafts and documents still in routing don't count.

In-Person Board Prep

Conversational and verification-focused: can you think critically and articulate your answer, drawing on the references and your own experience?
One question per Major Performance Area, drawn from a bank of five — every candidate gets the same starting questions, and the starting bank is public (JFHQ SharePoint, see Useful Links).
Each answer is scored on comprehension (understood the question, stayed on topic), clarity (your own position, concise), relevance (supported by references, data, or experience), appropriateness (matches the leadership level of the rank), and rationale (reasonable, has merit).
Also graded: dress & appearance (clean, serviceable uniform; grooming; accouterments properly worn) and professionalism (composure, straight posture, no rocking, correct facing movements, eye contact with every board member).
The board's final call is a single yes/no: ready for the duties and responsibilities of the rank?

Board reporting instructions and the full question bank live on the JFHQ SharePoint (Useful Links — CAC required).

Point values mirror the NJANG Enlisted Promotion Documentation Review scoresheets (HQ NJANG IMT 006–010) as briefed in the NJANG Enlisted Promotion Process update of 14 Jan 2026. Only items documented on official, finalized records count — the vMPF record review and MyFitness individual fitness tracker; no drafts or documents still in routing. The objective data above is scored before your package reaches the panel; a three-member panel then scores the subjective portion (Enlisted Performance Brief and NJANG IMT 004, four sections at 7.5 pts each). A total documentation-review score of 70 or higher is the recommended promotion standard, and candidates for SSgt, MSgt, and CMSgt also meet an in-person board. This is an unofficial aid — verify against the official scoresheets on the NJANG JFHQ SharePoint and talk to your supervisor or the training office. Nothing entered here is saved or sent anywhere.
